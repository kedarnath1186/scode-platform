The Challenge

Why Most Local Businesses Are Still Offline

Many local businesses still don't have a website because they believe it's out of reach. Here's what holds them back.

💰

Too Expensive

Small businesses assume that getting a website requires a large upfront investment for design, development, domain registration, and ongoing hosting fees.

🧩

Too Complicated

The process of registering domains, configuring DNS, choosing hosting providers, and managing servers feels overwhelming for non-technical business owners.

⚙️

Requires Tech Knowledge

Many believe they need coding skills or IT expertise to maintain a website, update content, or handle security — making them hesitant to even start.
